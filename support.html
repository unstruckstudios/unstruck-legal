Common questions

How do I move a player to another device?

Make sure Online is turned on for that player (Settings → Online). Then go to Play, tap the player to edit them, and choose My Sync Code. On the new device, choose Sync a Player and enter the code.

What does Online do?

Online lets a player sync their progress across devices and join the public leaderboards. Offline keeps everything on this device only. You can switch at any time in Settings. When you turn Online on, all of the player's game data syncs at that time.

How do I get on (or off) the leaderboard?

Turn on Online, then open the Leaderboard and use the "Show me on the public leaderboard" switch. Turning it off removes the player from the public boards.

I deleted a player by mistake.

If the player was Online and you have their sync code, choose Sync a Player and enter the code to bring them back. Offline players can't be recovered after they're deleted.

How do I delete a player's data?

Delete the player in the app, then email us their player name and ask us to remove their online data.
