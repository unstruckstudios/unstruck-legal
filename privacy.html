Privacy Policy

FlashDeck: Academy - Math

Effective October 12, 2026 · Last updated October 12, 2026

FlashDeck: Academy - Math ("the app") is made by Bryan N. Green, doing business as Unstruck Studios ("we," "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. It is written to be read by parents.

The short version: the app does not ask for a real name, email address, phone number, or location. It does not show ads. It does not track your child across other apps or websites, and it does not sell or share data with anyone for advertising. A player can play fully offline, and their nickname and points appear on the public leaderboards only if they choose to join.

What the app collects

For a player who chooses Online, the app sends our servers only what it needs to save progress, move it between devices, and run the optional leaderboards. A player who chooses Offline sends nothing; everything stays on the device.

  • Player name (gamertag). A nickname the player types in. It is meant to be a made-up name, not a real one. It is checked by our servers to make sure it is not already taken and not inappropriate. It is shown to other players only if the player joins the public leaderboards (see below).
  • Game progress. Levels completed, scores, total points, Time Trial best scores, coins, stars, achievements, unlocked items, and similar in-game results.
  • A random account number. Generated by the app to keep one player's progress separate from another's. It is not tied to any personal identity and is never shown publicly.
  • A sync code. A short code used to move a player's saved progress to another device.
  • Last activity time. When the player's progress was last saved to our servers.

Online and Offline play

When a player is created, the app asks whether to play Online or Offline. Nothing is sent to our servers until that choice is made. The choice can be changed at any time in Settings:

  • Offline: the player's name and progress stay on the device only. No sync code is available and the player is not on the public leaderboards. Offline players can still view the leaderboards.
  • Online: the player's progress is saved to our servers so it can be loaded on another device with a sync code. Switching from Offline to Online sends the player's saved progress at that time.

Switching a player to Offline stops all further sending and removes the player from the public leaderboards.

Public leaderboards (optional)

The app has public leaderboards for total points and for each level's Time Trial. Joining is off by default. A player appears on them only if they are Online, turn on "Show me on the public leaderboard," and pass a date-of-birth check.

When a player has joined, other players can see only:

  • Their gamertag
  • Their total points
  • Their best Time Trial score and number of correct answers for each level

No avatar, account number, sync code, or any other information is shown. Turning the switch off removes the player from all public leaderboards.

Date-of-birth checks

Before showing a sync code or letting a player join the public leaderboards, the app asks for a date of birth. It is used only on the device, at that moment, to decide whether the feature is available. It is not saved and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Parent progress report

After a level, a parent can create a progress report. It is made on the device and shared only through the device's own share options (for example, Messages or email) to whoever the parent chooses. We never receive it, and the app does not keep a copy.

What the app does not collect

  • Real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses
  • Location, photos, camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Advertising identifiers or any cross-app tracking data
  • Payment card information

The app contains no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software.

Please don't enter real names

The player name field is for a nickname. We ask that players not enter a real full name, a school name, or anything else that identifies them. The app filters obvious inappropriate words, but it cannot detect a real name, so this is worth mentioning to your child.

How information is used

Only to run the app: to save progress, display it back to the player, restore it on a new device, and show the public leaderboards for players who joined them. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any form of marketing.

Where information is stored

Saved progress is stored on the device and, for Online players, on servers operated by Supabase, a hosting provider acting on our behalf. Supabase may only process this information to provide hosting to us; it may not use it for its own purposes.

Information is kept while the app is in use and deleted on request (see below).

Purchases

FlashDeck: Academy - Math is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or receive payment card details. Coins and stars earned in the game are part of the game only — they cannot be bought, have no cash value, and cannot be exchanged for money or transferred out of the app.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for elementary-age learners and is intended to be used with a parent or guardian's knowledge. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The nickname, progress data, sync code, and random account number described above are used solely to support the app's internal operations, and are never used to contact a child, advertise to a child, or build a profile of a child. Features that share information beyond the device (sync codes and the public leaderboards) are behind a date-of-birth check, and the public leaderboards are off unless a player turns them on.

Parents and guardians may, at any time:

  • Ask what information is associated with their child's saved progress
  • Ask that it be deleted
  • Refuse to allow further collection by deleting the app

To make any of these requests, email unstruckstudios@gmail.com. Please include the sync code shown in the app so we can find the right record. We will respond within 30 days.

Deleting saved data

Deleting the app, or deleting a player in the app, removes the data stored on the device. Turning off "Show me on the public leaderboard" (or switching the player to Offline) removes them from the public leaderboards. To also remove saved progress stored on our servers, email unstruckstudios@gmail.com with the sync code.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, to protect saved progress. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting children's information will be made before, not after, the change takes effect.
