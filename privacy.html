Privacy Policy

FlashDeck: Academy - Math

Effective August 21, 2026 · Last updated August 21, 2026

FlashDeck: Academy - Math ("the app") is made by Bryan N. Green, doing business as Unstruck Studios ("we," "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what choices you have. It is written to be read by parents.

The short version: the app does not ask for a real name, email address, phone number, or location. It does not show ads. It does not track your child across other apps or websites, and it does not sell or share data with anyone for advertising.

What the app collects

The app collects only what it needs to save a player's progress and move it between devices:

  • Player name (gamertag). A nickname the player types in. It is meant to be a made-up name, not a real one, and it is only shown on that player's own device.
  • Game progress. Levels completed, scores, coins, stars, and similar in-game results.
  • A random account number. Generated by the app to keep one player's progress separate from another's. It is not tied to any personal identity.
  • A sync code. A short code used to move a player's saved progress to another device.

What the app does not collect

  • Real names, email addresses, phone numbers, or mailing addresses
  • Location, photos, camera, microphone, or contacts
  • Advertising identifiers or any cross-app tracking data
  • Payment card information

The app contains no third-party advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software.

Please don't enter real names

The player name field is for a nickname. We ask that players not enter a real full name, a school name, or anything else that identifies them. The app filters obvious inappropriate words, but it cannot detect a real name, so this is worth mentioning to your child.

How information is used

Only to run the app: to save progress, display it back to the player, and restore it on a new device. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any form of marketing.

Where information is stored

Saved progress is stored on the device and on servers operated by Supabase, a hosting provider acting on our behalf. Supabase may only process this information to provide hosting to us; it may not use it for its own purposes.

Information is kept while the app is in use and deleted on request (see below).

Purchases

FlashDeck: Academy - Math is a paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see or receive payment card details. Coins and stars earned in the game are part of the game only — they cannot be bought, have no cash value, and cannot be exchanged for money or transferred out of the app.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for elementary-age learners and is intended to be used with a parent or guardian's knowledge. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The nickname, progress data, sync code, and random account number described above are used solely to support the app's internal operations, and are never used to contact a child, advertise to a child, or build a profile of a child.

Parents and guardians may, at any time:

  • Ask what information is associated with their child's saved progress
  • Ask that it be deleted
  • Refuse to allow further collection by deleting the app

To make any of these requests, email unstruckstudios@gmail.com. Please include the sync code shown in the app so we can find the right record. We will respond within 30 days.

Deleting saved data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on the device. To also remove data stored on our servers, email unstruckstudios@gmail.com with the sync code.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections, to protect saved progress. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or how we use it, we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes affecting children's information will be made before, not after, the change takes effect.
